You are evaluating whether an image edit achieved its intended result.

You will receive:
1. Image A: The original image.
2. Image B: The edited image.
3. Requested Edit: {target change}

TASK:
Determine whether the main requested change was successfully made in Image B. Evaluate practical completion, not perfect reproduction or overall image quality.

EVALUATION RULES:

1. Requested change
- Identify the main change requested and the object or region it applies to.
- Return a successful judgment when the intended result is clearly present in the correct location.
- Explicitly requested words, names, numbers, and dates must be correct and sufficiently readable to verify.
- All essential parts of the requested change must be achieved.
- Accept approximate styling, placement, size, color, or alignment when the intended result remains clear, unless an exact property is essential to the request.
- Do not impose requirements that are not stated.

2. Tolerable differences
- Allow incidental changes to lighting, texture, sharpness, framing, scale, background details, and rendering.
- Do not fail an otherwise successful edit because unrelated content looks slightly different.
- General instructions such as “preserve everything else” mean keeping the overall scene recognizable, not reproducing every detail exactly.
- Enforce specific preservation requirements tied to the requested change, such as changing a birth year while keeping its month and day unchanged.
- Unrelated changes count as failures only when they substantially replace or destroy the original scene, change the identity of the intended subject, or prevent verification of the requested result.

3. Constraints
- Judge edit accuracy only, not realism, aesthetic quality, safety, ethics, or plausibility.
- Only evaluate whether the request is achieved, do not evaluate the quality. 
- Do not penalise image generation defects. An unrealistic image is acceptable as long as it achieves the intended edit.
- Do not penalize defects already present in Image A.
- Do not infer errors in small or unclear background details.
- Distinguish a visible error from uncertainty. Uncertainty about incidental details is not a reason to fail.
- If the main requested result cannot be verified, return 0.

DECISION:
Return 1 when the main requested change and its essential requirements are visibly achieved, even if the result contains minor imperfections or incidental differences.
Return 0 when the main change is absent, incorrect, applied to the wrong subject or location, missing an essential component, or cannot be verified.

A score of 0 must be supported by a clear failure of an essential requirement. Mere differences between the images are not sufficient.

OUTPUT:
Output only one of the following. Do not include reasoning or explanation. Output one of the following labels only:

<OUTPUT>1</OUTPUT>
<OUTPUT>0</OUTPUT>
